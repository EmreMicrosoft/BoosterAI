## Durable direct verification denial

The harness attempts to persist a direct PermissionError separately from process outcome inside its verification try block. Proven prelaunch or successfully returned/reaped-child denials become DENIED; a potentially live child retains AMBIGUOUS and its executor slot. A pending STOP retains STOP_REQUESTED and the project stop barrier (status exposes AMBIGUOUS), never asserting that termination succeeded. Durable access_denial metadata prohibits replay and reconciliation; unknown scratch effects and the original exception are preserved. A failed initial or STOP state write is not retried just to record denial. If recording denial fails, the error explicitly reports that persistence was not confirmed; the previous state can remain VERIFYING. A future host must retain and obey the original retry prohibition: persistent state alone cannot retain a denial when writing it is unavailable. Denials before this try block and during the final result write remain outside this narrow correction. No universal refusal detector or durable-storage guarantee is claimed.

Four injected-denial regressions and seven existing prerequisite-recovery regressions passed on Windows/Python 3.14. The original denial remains the exception cause; status, reconcile and verify reject replay after a successfully persisted DENIED state. Existing retained scratch was unchanged.
